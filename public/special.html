How it works

Every draw serial is divided by a chosen divisor and the remainder (phase) groups draws into cycle buckets. A grid search tested all divisors from 2 to 43 over 200 back-test draws — divisor 25 scored the highest average matches (1.055 vs random baseline 0.837). The prediction picks the 6 most frequent numbers from the same-phase-25 historical draws.
