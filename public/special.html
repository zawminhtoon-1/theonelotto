Loto 6 — Modular Cycle Prediction

Special method · 200-draw backtest

How it works

Every draw serial is divided by 43 (the total number pool). The remainder — the phase — groups draws into 43 cycle buckets. The prediction looks at all past draws sharing the same phase as the next draw, then picks the 6 most frequent numbers from that subset. The idea: if the lottery follows any 43-draw cycle, this method captures it.
