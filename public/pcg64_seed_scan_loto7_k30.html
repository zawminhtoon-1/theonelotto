PCG64 companion to the Loto7 xoshiro256** K=30 scan — testing whether swapping the underlying PRNG family (not just K) changes anything descriptively, and the Loto7 counterpart to the Loto6 PCG64 K=38 scan. Same draw window #1–650 (650 draws) and combined-seed formula (seed×10,000,000 + draw_serial) as the xoshiro K=30 scan, but a much wider seed range — -5,000,000 to 5,000,000 (10,000,001 values, split into 10 stages of ~1,000,000 each, matching the Loto6 PCG64 K=38 scan's range and staging convention rather than the xoshiro scan's narrower ±1,000,000).

How the seed becomes picks: the combined seed is expanded into PCG64's raw 128-bit {state, inc} via SplitMix64 run four times (the same expansion primitive the xoshiro scans use for their 256-bit state, just producing 128+128 bits instead of 4×64 — this site's own seeding convention, chosen to stay JS-portable). Each next-value call then runs O'Neill's standard PCG64 XSL-RR construction: a 128-bit LCG advance (state = state×0x2360ed051fc65da44385df649fccf645 + inc (mod 2¹²&sup8;)) followed by a 64-bit xor-shift + random-rotate output permutation. K=30 picks come from the same partial Fisher-Yates over range(1,38) (37-number pool, not 44) as every other Loto7 scan on this site.

Verification: the pure-Python core (already proven bit-exact against numpy.random.Generator(PCG64()) for the Loto6 pool_max=43 case) was re-verified for this pool_max=37/K=30 configuration specifically — direct low-level state injection (bypassing SeedSequence) driving NumPy's actual bit generator and replicating the identical Fisher-Yates loop on its raw 64-bit words, confirmed matching exactly before this scan was ever run. Self-checked per stage against independently-computed known-good reference vectors (including negative seeds) before scaling, same discipline as every other scan on this site.

Five metrics tracked per seed: hit7b = draws where the 30 picks contain all 7 main winning numbers and at least one of the 2 bonus numbers (either bonus, same convention as the xoshiro K=30 Loto7 page); hit7 = all 7 main numbers (any bonus); hit6/hit5/hit4 = exactly 6/5/4 of 7. At K=30 (a 30-of-37 pool, ~81% coverage) chance rates run fairly high — hypergeometric expectation is hit7b≈122.32, hit7≈128.5, hit6≈262.4, hit5≈188.9, hit4≈60.6 per seed (of 650 draws) — all five tiers are tracked for the same ranking convention: highest hit7b, tiebreak hit7, hit6, hit5, hit4.
