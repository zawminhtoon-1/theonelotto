The backtest applies this exact three-way construction to three windows — #2084–2133 (50 draws), #2033–2133 (101 draws), and #1884–2133 (250 draws) — re-deriving the pool fresh for every target (no leakage), and tallies hit6b (6 main + bonus), hit6 (6 main, any bonus, equivalent to full containment), hit5, and hit4 against the hypergeometric chance expectation — computed per draw using that draw's actual pool size (28–33 across these windows, not a fixed average), then summed for the significance test.

⚠️ Honest framing: in the 101-draw and 50-draw windows, none of the four hit tiers reach conventional statistical significance (all p > 0.05). The wider 250-draw window (#1884–2133, added 2026-09-03) shows one exception — hit6/containment comes in at 33 observed vs. 23.59 expected (1.40×, p=0.0413), just clearing 0.05. Given how much of that window overlaps the seeds' own selection window (see the caveat below) and that this is one tier out of many tested across this whole site, treat it as a borderline, unconfirmed observation, not a validated edge — the other three tiers in the same window, and all four tiers in the two narrower windows, sit at chance.

In-sample seed selection caveat: the PCG64 seed #-4,675,555 was found by scanning against the fixed #1–2050 draw window. This matters more for wider backtest windows, since they reach further back into that same range:

  • 50-draw window (#2084–2133): 0 of 50 draws in-sample — fully clean, out-of-sample throughout.
  • 101-draw window (#2033–2133): 18 of 101 draws in-sample (#2033–2050).
  • 250-draw window (#1884–2133): 167 of 250 draws in-sample (#1884–2050) — a majority of this window overlaps the seed's own selection window, so its results (including the borderline hit6 figure above) deserve the most skepticism of the three.

The xoshiro seed #692,809 has an analogous history from its own earlier K=38 scan. Treat every window with the usual skepticism, and the in-sample portions specifically with a bit extra.
