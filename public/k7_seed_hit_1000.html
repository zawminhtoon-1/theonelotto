Distribution of hitting seeds (covered draws)

Histogram of the 915 recorded first-hitting seeds, binned in 100,000-wide buckets across the full ±1,000,000 range.

Negative-seed bins account for 687 of the 915 hits (75.1%), positive-seed bins for 228 (24.9%). This lean toward negative seeds is a scan-order artifact, not a property of the RNG: each draw's "hitting seed" is the first one found scanning ascending from −1,000,000, so a negative seed is recorded whenever it happens to work before any positive seed is even tried — it says nothing about which seeds are more likely to hit.
